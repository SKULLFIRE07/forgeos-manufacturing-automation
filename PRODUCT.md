# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ForgeOS serves manufacturing workers, supervisors, project managers, department heads, quality teams, safety teams, maintenance teams, HR and administration, auditors, and plant leadership. Workers use shared kiosks or tablets during active operations. Supervisors use tablets and desktops during shift planning, execution, handover, and review. Managers and leadership use desktop command views to identify delivery risk, unblock teams, verify closure, and improve factory performance.

## Product Purpose

ForgeOS is the manufacturing operating system between ERP planning and real factory execution. It connects customer commitments, projects, work orders, operations, people, machines, materials, documents, inspections, issues, corrective actions, evidence, and KPIs in one accountable operational graph. Success means every important activity has a visible owner, due time, current state, escalation path, and verified proof of closure. Dashboards are generated from work instead of manually prepared after the fact.

## Brand Personality

Disciplined, decisive, dependable. The product should feel calm under pressure, exact without feeling bureaucratic, and authoritative without becoming intimidating. Its voice is direct, plain, and action oriented.

## Anti-references

ForgeOS must not resemble a generic SaaS card wall, a decorative analytics demo, a dense legacy ERP clone, a dark-only control room, or a collection of disconnected forms. It must avoid vanity metrics, manual status theater, color-only communication, excessive animation, hidden ownership, unclear actions, and interfaces that expose every module to every role.

## Design Principles

1. Operational truth before reporting. Every number must trace back to real work, evidence, or an approved system of record.
2. Accountability is always visible. Every action shows its owner, verifier, due time, state, and escalation path.
3. The platform contains everything, but each person sees only what helps them act now.
4. Completion requires proof. Critical work is submitted, verified, and then closed with an immutable history.
5. Exceptions lead the interface. Blocked, overdue, unsafe, nonconforming, or at-risk work appears before healthy work.
6. Integrate instead of duplicate. ERP, HRMS, biometric, and machine systems remain authoritative where appropriate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use high contrast black, white, and gray. Distinguish priority, risk, status, and success through words, icons, line styles, and fill patterns. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen readers, reduced motion, zoom, large touch targets, and clear error recovery. The information architecture and content model must support localization and longer translated labels. Shop floor workflows must remain usable on shared touch devices and in unreliable network conditions.
